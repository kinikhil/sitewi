B. Optometry

Course Description

The undergraduate programme B. Optometry is a four-year UGC recognised degree course under Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT).

Optometry is a health care profession which deals with the examination, diagnosis, treatment and management of diseases and disorders of the visual system. It is a vision care science. One can also define it as the science of eye equipment (including lenses and spectacles) which is imbued with the idea of improving the vision of the human eye and remove all kinds of obstacles of sight which an individual may experience. This is a dynamic and challenging career, which allows one to help people, achieve personal growth, community respect, job flexibility and financial success and offers virtually unlimited opportunities.

Program Overview

B. Optometry programme at NIMAS is unique in its objective to:

  • Develop a multipurpose ophthalmic manpower at paramedical level
  • Enable a student to become a competent person in providing service as an Optician, Optometrist, Refractionist and Ophthalmic Assistant to the community in urban, semi-urban and rural settings in private, semi-Governmental and Governmental sectors

Eligibility Criteria

  • Students who are due to appear for Class XII final exams/awaiting results may be given provisional admission depending on their CET score
  • Admissions are strictly on merit basis

Curriculum (Course Modules)

  • Professional Communication in English
  • Anatomy (General & Ocular)
  • Physiology (General & Ocular)
  • Geometrical & Physical Optics
  • Biochemistry (General & Ocular)
  • Computers, Environment & Ecology
  • Visual Optics & Lighting
  • Medical Pathology & Microbiology
  • Pharmacology
  • Ophthalmic & Optical Instrumentation
  • Clinical Refraction & Ocular Diseases
  • Dispensing Optics & Contact Lenses
  • Low Vision Aids & Visual Rehabilitation
  • Public Health, Biostatistics & Practice Management
  • Practical Training (Clinical Optometry, Low Vision, Dispensing & Contact Lens)
