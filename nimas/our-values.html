Our Values

Our vision is to be one of the most influential institutes in West Bengal and India.

We believe in a remarkable future, in doing our best and supporting others to do theirs.

We know professional and industrial environments constantly vary and that institutes needed to equip their students with the skills to adapt and lead positive change for the future.

Our mission is to engage in outstanding education that makes a major contribution to the future of the country. Students will be provided with an excellent education and the capacity to develop and apply knowledge to exercise influence and make meaningful life-long contributions to their communities.

Values and Commitments:

In pursuit of our vision and mission, the institute is committed to:

  • Engaging students as partners in their education
  • Develop leadership, teamwork and entrepreneurship
  • To foster a global perspective
  • Recognizing and valuing diversity
  • To act with integrity
  • To engage with industry and educational partners
